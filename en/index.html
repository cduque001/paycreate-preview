03 / KNOW YOUR OPTIONS

Your fees. In plain English.

The right approach depends on your business. We help you understand the differences before you decide.

Availability, pricing, and program requirements depend on your business and location. Your review explains the applicable terms.

01

Interchange-plus

A pricing structure that separates underlying interchange and network costs from the processor’s markup. Review the full fee picture, including other account charges.

02

Dual pricing

Displays separate cash and card prices. Ask how customer-facing prices, equipment, and applicable requirements would work for your business.

03

Surcharging

A fee on eligible credit-card transactions, subject to network rules and local requirements. Debit cards and other restrictions need separate consideration.

04

Cash discount

Offers a discount from the posted price for eligible cash payments. The way prices and discounts are displayed matters; discuss the details before choosing.
