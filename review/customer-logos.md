# Customer logo provenance

These ten marks come from the “Trusted by Clients Like You” section on the
[existing PayCreate restaurant page](https://www.paycreate.co/restaurant-solutions),
checked October 16, 2026. The creator requested their reuse on the homepage.
Each file is a byte-for-byte copy of its original Wix asset; no logo was generated,
redrawn, recolored, or replaced. Names were checked against the artwork.

The manifest is `lib/marketing/customer-logos.ts`; files live in
`public/images/customers/`. Original files total approximately 220 KB. Their
dimensions and contain-fit styling preserve proportions; small originals remain
small enough to avoid obvious enlargement.
