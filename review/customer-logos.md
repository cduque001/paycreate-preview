# Customer logo provenance

These ten marks come from the “Trusted by Clients Like You” section on the
[existing PayCreate restaurant page](https://www.paycreate.co/restaurant-solutions),
checked October 16, 2026. The creator requested their reuse on the homepage.
Nine files are byte-for-byte copies of the original Wix assets. At the creator's
request, Juice Generation now uses a crisp full-logo SVG from
[American Mural's customer showcase](https://americanmural.com/), replacing the
small horizontal Wix image. The supplied vector is copied intact; no artwork was
generated, redrawn, or recolored. Names were checked against the artwork.

The manifest is `lib/marketing/customer-logos.ts`; files live in
`public/images/customers/`. Contain-fit styling preserves each mark's proportions.
The homepage now centers larger logos without visible captions; descriptive image
alt text keeps customer names available to screen readers. Juice Generation leads
the list with a slightly larger mark and a subtle green card border.
