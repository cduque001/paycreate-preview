# Photography sources

Selected and downloaded October 13, 2026. These are real photographs, not
AI-generated images. The four new assets are hosted in `public/images/business/`
and delivered through Next.js Image with responsive sizes and lazy loading.
Service hero images are prioritized; all image containers reserve their height.
Framing uses CSS `object-fit` and subject-specific `object-position` values.

existing partner relationship. No customer names, results, or quotes have been
