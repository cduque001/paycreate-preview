# Arena Sports — PayCreate × Blismo proposal

[Open the mobile proposal](https://cduque001.github.io/paycreate-preview/collateral/arena-sports/)

[Download the one-page PDF](https://cduque001.github.io/paycreate-preview/collateral/arena-sports/PayCreate_Blismo_Arena_Sports_Proposal.pdf)

Prepared for Arena Sports, 1502 NJ-35, Ocean Township, NJ 07712.

Quoted offer: Blismo $70.00/month for 3 users; PayCreate processing at a proposed 2.5%; a free snack bar point of sale register; and a Dejavoo payment device for the front desk. Final scope, processing terms, equipment models, and activation timing are confirmed at setup.

The design retains the approved PayCreate × Blismo branding and emerald/violet device displays, with Arena-specific copy and an AI-generated sports venue concept. The venue image is illustrative, not a photograph of Arena Sports.

The Arena logo is from the [USPTO trademark image for serial 50029923](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/img/50029923/large), associated with an Arena Sports LLC in Ocean, NJ. Matching this mark to the recipient is an inference from the company name, township, and shopping-center address range; an official venue website was not confirmed.

Device images are representative Dejavoo D1 and P1 product photography. Blismo branding comes from its official site. Font licenses are included in assets.
